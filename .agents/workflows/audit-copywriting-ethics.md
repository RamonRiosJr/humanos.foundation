# Copywriting & Ethics Auditor

**Objective:** Audit all public-facing text, ensuring the Foundation never sounds "broke," desperate, or legally vulnerable. 

## 1. The Desperation Filter
- **Zero Begging:** If a page asks for money in a way that compromises the $3.0M Capitalization valuation, flag it immediately.
- **Vaporware Checks:** Do we claim features that do not exist yet? Rewrite them as "Pipeline" or "Architecture Goals" so we don't cross into fraud.

## 2. The Credibility & Ethics Anchor
- **Ethical Friction:** Are we listing "Partners" or "Sponsors" who have not officially signed MOUs? If so, purge the names. Do not logo-jack federal entities like Federal Health Equity or HL7.
- **Tone:** The tone should be highly educated, resolute, and inspiring. We use words like "Sovereignty," "Zero-Knowledge," "Mathematical Guarantee," and "Ecosystem."

## 3. Structural Review
- Examine the `.jsx` files and `en.json` translation files. 
- Ensure that the messaging connects specifically to Federal Health Equity federal grant language and B2B enterprise logic without sounding "ridiculous."

**Execution:** Rewrite offending copy immediately and issue a warning to the Founder explaining why their original draft compromised the foundation's valuation logic.
